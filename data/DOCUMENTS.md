## Minimum for the Phase 1 demo (download these first)

1. PMFBY (crop insurance) guidelines - covers the crop damage case
2. PMMVY (pregnancy benefit) guidelines - covers the pregnancy benefit case
3. PM-Kisan guidelines - covers the "Do I qualify with 2 acres?" eligibility rules
