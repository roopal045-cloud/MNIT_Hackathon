## PMFBY reading notes (read 30-10-2026)
- Issuer (title page): Department of Agriculture, Cooperation and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Krishi Bhawan, New Delhi.
- Version date: the text only says "(Revised)", no date. NEEDS HUMAN VERIFICATION (check pmfby.gov.in for the newest version).
- Download address: NEEDS HUMAN VERIFICATION (the file has no download record). The page footer prints www.pmfby.gov.in.
- Localized risks (para 5.1.4 and 21.5): hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst, natural fire due to lightning. Farmer must report within 72 hours. Payout within 15 days of the loss assessment. Premium must be paid before the peril.
- Inundation counts only with visible crop damage. Mere water logging does not count.
- Farmer premium (Table 1, para 13.1): Kharif annual commercial crops, 5% of sum insured or actuarial rate, whichever is less. Whether cotton in Maharashtra falls in this class, and the state sum insured: NEEDS HUMAN VERIFICATION.
- Documents for non-loanee farmers (para 35.10): Aadhaar or enrolment ID, land record, bank passbook, sowing certificate or self-declaration. DHARA must only list these in the checklist, never collect them.
- The document mentions a central toll-free number for claim intimation but does not print it. NEEDS HUMAN VERIFICATION.
- This document has no land-size limit.
